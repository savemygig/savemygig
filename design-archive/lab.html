Three habits. Zero lost gigs.

Two identical drives

Same export on both, every gig. One in the bag, one in your pocket. Redundancy beats recovery, every time.

Clean exports only

FAT32 + MBR, exported fresh from Rekordbox after every library change. Never copy an old database onto a new drive.

Eject like a pro

From Rekordbox and from the player. The unsafe eject is the #1 silent killer of gig drives.
