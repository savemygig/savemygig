DJM-900NXS2
DJM-900NXS2, the field manual
Reviewed: 26 July 2026
×
Booth briefing. A 4-channel professional
 mixer with 2 USB ports (built for back-to-back
 DJs to hot-swap without cutting the music), 14 Beat FX
 types and 6 Sound Color FX on every channel.
 It is the standard pairing for the CDJ-2000NXS2 and still one of the most
 common mixers in professional booths. Before you touch it: know where the
 channel gain trim is before the set starts, not during it, clipping on this
 desk is a gain-staging problem, not an effects problem.

 Emergency fixes
A channel has no signal
Short answer. Before assuming the channel has failed,
 check the channel fader, the crossfader assignment, and the input selector,
 in that order.
Why this happens. The channel fader can simply be down,
 the crossfader assign switch can have that channel routed to the opposite
 side you are pushing, or the input selector can be set to a source that is
 not actually playing (LINE instead of USB, for example).
Fix it now. Bring the channel fader fully up. Check the
 crossfader assign switch (LEFT / THRU / RIGHT) matches where your crossfader
 actually is. Confirm the input selector for that channel matches your source.
 If all three check out, move the source to a different channel to isolate
 whether the problem is the channel strip or upstream.
Avoid it next time. Do a full gain and routing check on
 every channel before doors open, not after the first complaint from the
 booth.

Faders behaving unexpectedly
Short answer. This mixer uses Magvel magnetic, contact-free
 faders, so wear-related scratchiness is rare, but a mismatched fader curve
 setting is a common cause of a channel that feels "wrong."
Why this happens. The channel fader and crossfader curve
 are both independently adjustable, a curve set for scratching feels
 completely different from one set for smooth blending, and a previous DJ may
 have left it set for their own style.
Fix it now. Check the fader curve selector switches on
 the channel strips and crossfader before your set, and reset them to your
 own preference rather than assuming the default.
Avoid it next time. If you are playing back-to-back,
 agree curve settings with the other DJ before the handover, not mid-set.

Effects not engaging
Short answer. If Beat FX or Sound Color FX will not
 engage, the channel select for the effect almost always does not match the
 channel you are trying to affect.
Why this happens. Beat FX and Sound Color FX are both
 assigned to a specific channel (or the master) independently of which channel
 fader is up, it is easy to dial in an effect on the wrong assignment under
 pressure.
Fix it now. Confirm the FX channel select matches the
 channel you want affected, confirm the FX level/depth knob is not at zero,
 and for Beat FX confirm the BPM (tap or auto) is reading correctly, a wrong
 BPM reading makes a beat-synced effect sound broken even when it is working.
Avoid it next time. Set your FX channel assignment as part
 of your pre-set routine, the same moment you check gain and routing.

Headphones or cue not working
Short answer. Check the cue/PFL button on the channel
 before assuming a hardware fault, cueing has to be switched on per channel.
Why this happens. Each channel has its own cue button;
 it is easy to be listening to the wrong channel entirely, especially when
 swapping decks quickly.
Fix it now. Press cue/PFL on the channel you actually
 want to hear, check the headphone level and mix (cue/master blend) knobs are
 not at zero, and try the other headphone jack if the mixer has more than one.
Avoid it next time. Set your headphone mix and level as
 part of your pre-set check, not after you have already started cueing the
 next track.

USB sound card not recognized by the computer
Short answer. With 2 USB ports built
 for DJ hand-offs, confirm which port your software is actually pointed at
 before assuming a fault.
Why this happens. DJ software has to be told which USB
 audio device to use, and with two ports available it is easy for the audio
 settings to still be pointed at the previous DJ's connection.
Fix it now. In your software's audio preferences, confirm
 the DJM-900NXS2 is selected as the input and output device, and that you are
 plugged into the port matching your channel assignment. Unplug and replug
 the USB cable directly, skip hubs.
Avoid it next time. Check your channel and USB port
 assignment as soon as you plug in, before you are relying on it live.

 Features explained
Beat FX
Short answer. 14 beat-synced effect
 types, selected and dialed in via the X-Pad with its own OLED display.
Why it matters. Beat FX locks the effect's timing to the
 track's BPM automatically, so a delay or echo lands musically without manual
 tapping.
When professionals use it. A short echo out on the last
 bar before a mix, or a filter sweep to build tension into a drop.
Common mistakes. Leaving an effect engaged after the
 transition and forgetting it is still active, and trusting an incorrect BPM
 read instead of confirming it against the track.
Related: Sound Color FXEffects not engaging
Sound Color FX
Short answer. Six always-available effects on every
 channel: Sweep, Filter, Crush, Dub Echo, Noise, Space, controlled by a single knob
 per channel, no menu diving.
Why it matters. Because it lives on the channel strip
 itself, it is fast enough to use mid-mix without looking away from the
 crowd or the next track.
When professionals use it. Filter into a breakdown, or a
 touch of Space to open up a track before a big drop.
Common mistakes. Overusing Crush or Noise on a main room
 system, they read as dramatic on headphones and can sound harsh through a
 full PA.
Related: Beat FX
Dual USB
Short answer. 2 USB ports built into
 the mixer, so two DJs (or one DJ and a backup laptop) can be plugged in and
 ready at the same time.
Why it matters. The outgoing DJ does not have to unplug
 before the incoming DJ can prepare, the handover is a channel and fader move,
 not a cable scramble.
When professionals use it. Any back-to-back set, and any
 night where you want a laptop plugged in as backup while running from CDJs.
Common mistakes. Assuming the mixer auto-selects the
 right USB input, confirm your software's audio device and the channel input
 selector agree, see USB not recognized.

 Professional tips
Gain staging before the room, not during it
Short answer. Set channel trim so the signal sits around
 unity on the meter before you touch an effect or an EQ.
Why it matters. Clipping on this mixer is a gain problem
 far more often than an effects problem. Chasing distortion with EQ or FX
 adjustments while the actual cause is trim set too hot wastes time and rarely
 fixes it.
Do this. Set trim per channel while cueing, before the
 track is live in the room, so you are not adjusting gain in front of a
 crowd.
Common mistakes. Copying another DJ's trim setting
 without checking it, different tracks and different software output levels
 need different trim.

 Preventive maintenance
Keeping the desk reliable over years of bookings
Short answer. Cable and connector care matters more than
 fader care on this mixer, the Magvel faders are contact-free by design.
Why it matters. RCA, USB and Ethernet connectors take the
 real wear over a touring life, loose or bent pins cause the intermittent
 faults that are hardest to diagnose live.
Do this. Inspect cables before they go in the bag, not
 just when a fault appears. Unplug by the connector, never by pulling the
 cable. Keep the desk covered during transport.
Avoid it next time. Do not coil cables tightly around
 connectors, that is where strain damage starts.

 Compatibility
Software and player compatibility
Short answer. Supports rekordbox DVS, Serato DJ Pro and
 Traktor Pro, and pairs with any Pro DJ Link player, library format
 (Device Library or OneLibrary) does not affect the mixer itself.
Why it matters. The mixer's built-in network switch is
 what lets multiple CDJs share tempo and playback info over Pro DJ Link, that
 works the same whichever generation of player you connect.
Do this. Confirm your DVS or DJ software license
 supports this mixer's timecode/soundcard input before the gig, not at
 soundcheck.
