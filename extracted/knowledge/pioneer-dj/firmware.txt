FIRMWARE MATRIX
What's broken on that deck, by firmware version
Reviewed: 26 July 2026
Booth briefing. Read the firmware version off the unit,
 find it below, and you know what can bite you tonight. Every entry comes
 from AlphaTheta's own published change histories, 17 documented
 faults across 6 models. On gear you control, sit at or above
 the safe version shown for it. On gear you do not control, this tells you
 what to plan around.
Read more…Read less
Finding the version on the unit
 On a CDJ or DJM, the firmware version is in the utility menu, and on
 players it is also shown on the startup screen while the unit boots.
 Check it at soundcheck, not at doors. If a venue deck sits below the
 safe version, you cannot fix that on the night, but you can stop it
 surprising you.

 Clear at 3.20 or later
3.30
 Withdrawn by AlphaTheta. It added OneLibrary, then playlists failed to display for people exporting from older rekordbox. No music or data was deleted, and the player reverted to Device Library. Do not install this version.

How to read this
 A version in the "Fixed in" column is the release that resolved that
 fault. Anything below it still has the fault. Anything at or above it
 does not.
 The mid-set marker is ours,
 not AlphaTheta's. It flags faults whose documented symptom can interrupt
 a set already in progress, so you can tell a real risk from an
 inconvenience at a glance. Everything else on this page is the vendor's
 own wording, condensed.
 Faults only. Feature additions are left out on purpose, because a missing
 feature does not end a set. Models appear here once their change history
 has been read against the primary source, which is why this list is
 shorter than the catalogue. Found something we have wrong?
 Tell us and we will check it against the source.
