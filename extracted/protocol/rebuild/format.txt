Step 3 of 5
USB format
⚠️
This erases the drive completely. It is the one exception to the never-format rule, and it is safe only because tonight's tracks are now on the computer. Confirm the disk name and size before you touch anything, formatting the wrong disk is unrecoverable.
Right-click the USB drive → Format (or Disk Utility on Mac).
Windows only offers FAT32 up to 32GB in that dialog. Bigger stick: use the free guiformat tool, two minutes.
Format: FAT32, not exFAT, not NTFS. Older CDJs refuse exFAT.
Name: SAVEMYGIG (or anything you like)
Quick format: OK
Scheme: Master Boot Record (MBR)
⚠️
Do not remove the USB during formatting.
