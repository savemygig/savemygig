The master
Check the master
Raise MASTER to 12 o'clock. Raise BOOTH too.
Check nothing is muted, and nothing is plugged into the master insert.
What is the master insert
 A patch point on the back of the mixer where an outboard processor sits in the master signal. If a cable is plugged in and nothing sends audio back, the master goes silent with every fader up. If something is patched in, it is almost certainly the venue's: ask before you pull it. 
 Words for the tech: "There's something patched into the master insert. Is that live, or is it dead gear I can pull?"
