Restart the player
⚠️
Only if nobody is playing on this player right now. If another DJ is on, skip to the next step.
Remove your USB first, press USB STOP, wait for the light to stop blinking.
Power the player off. Wait 20 seconds.
Power back on, let it fully boot.
Insert the USB and wait, some drives take 30+ seconds to mount.
Does the player read the USB now?
 You follow these steps at your own risk. Formatting or erasing a drive can
