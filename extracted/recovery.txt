GET YOUR FILES BACK
The honest version: software recovers files in most cases where the drive still powers on. It can't help a physically dead drive. Here's the route, in order.
⚠️ Before anything: stop writing to the drive. Don't format it, don't "try one more export". Every write overwrites recoverable music.
Step 1 - Recovery software
If your computer detects the drive (even showing 0 files or asking to format - say no), software has a real chance. Both of these scan free first, so you see what's recoverable before paying.

Step 2 - When software can't do it
Drive not detected anywhere, no light, physical damage, water: that's hardware. A recovery lab opens the drive and reads the memory chips directly. Costs run $100-500+, so it's for irreplaceable files, your own unreleased music, not tracks you can re-download. Search for a data recovery lab in your city and ask for a free diagnosis first; serious labs quote before they charge.
Step 3 - Never pay for recovery twice
Recovery is what you do once. A backup system is what you do instead, it takes one evening to set up.
