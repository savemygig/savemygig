PLAYLISTS NOT SHOWING
ON THE CDJ?
Short version: if tracks play but playlists are missing, your music arrived, the Rekordbox database didn't. Either the export skipped it, the eject corrupted it, or the drive was filled by drag-and-drop instead of Rekordbox.
Why it happens
Files were dragged onto the USB in Finder/Explorer, that copies music, not the database. Playlists only exist after a Rekordbox EXPORT-mode sync.
The export was interrupted or ejected unsafely, the database is written near the end of a sync; pull early and tracks survive but playlists don't.
Mixed exports from two computers, a second computer's Rekordbox can overwrite the device database with its own (empty) view.
Very old player firmware meeting a new Rekordbox database version.
At the venue (5 minutes)
No laptop? Use SOURCE → USB → FOLDER, your music is still playable by folder, tonight is safe.
With a laptop: open Rekordbox in EXPORT mode, connect the USB, drag tonight's playlists onto the device, wait for the full sync, eject with the Rekordbox button.
Re-insert on the player and check the PLAYLIST browse tab, not FOLDER.
The permanent fix
One rule: music gets on gig drives only via Rekordbox export. Never Finder, never Explorer.
One computer owns the drive. If you must use two, let one be the only writer.
After every library change: re-export, eject properly, verify the playlists load before leaving home.
