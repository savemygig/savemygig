HOW BIG SHOULD A
DJ USB BE?
Short answer: 32-64GB, quality brand, and buy two. That's several thousand tracks, more music than you could play in a week of sets, with fast mounting and full FAT32 comfort.
The track math
MP3 320kbps: ~12MB per track → a 32GB drive holds ~2,500 tracks; 64GB ~5,000.
Lossless (AIFF/WAV): ~40-60MB per track → 32GB ≈ 600 tracks, 64GB ≈ 1,200, still 20+ hours of music.
A 4-hour set is 60-80 tracks. Even the lossless 32GB carries eight full sets of options.
Why huge drives are worse in booths
Mount time: players index the database on insert, a stuffed 256GB drive can leave you staring at a loading screen mid-changeover.
Browsing: 20,000 tracks on a CDJ screen is not a library, it's a haystack. Curation beats capacity.
Failure blast radius: when the everything-drive dies, everything dies. Two medium drives beat one giant one - the two-drive strategy →
What to actually buy
32 or 64GB from a major brand, metal body preferred (booth drinks happen), USB-A: it works on every player. USB-C ports exist only on the CDJ-3000X generation onward.
