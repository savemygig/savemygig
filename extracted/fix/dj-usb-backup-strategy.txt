Backup_Strategy
TWO DRIVES,
ZERO PANIC
The whole system in one sentence: two identical USB drives, the same export on both, kept in two different pockets. Everything else on this site exists for people who don't do this yet.
Why redundancy beats recovery
Every recovery technique, ours included, is a gamble played under pressure. A mirrored second drive is not a gamble: it's a fifteen-second swap while the room keeps dancing. The pros you never hear panic stories about aren't lucky. They carry the boring second drive.
The setup (one evening)
Buy two identical drives, same brand, same model, same size. Identical drives behave identically on players. The models we trust →
Format both:FAT32 + MBR. Step-by-step →
Export to both, back to back, in Rekordbox EXPORT mode. Same playlists, same order. Eject each with the Rekordbox button.
Label them. A and B, tape or marker. In a dark booth you want zero thinking.
The habit (per gig)
Library changed this week? Re-export to both drives. An outdated backup is a trap.
Drive A in the gig bag. Drive B on your keychain or jacket. Never the same pocket, never the same bag.
At the booth: play from A. If A misbehaves mid-set, eject, insert B, keep mixing. That's the whole emergency plan.
Once a month: back up the Rekordbox library itself (File → Library → Backup Library) to a third place, cloud or home drive.
The failure math
A quality drive fails rarely; call it once in hundreds of gig-nights. Two independent drives failing the same night is that number squared, effectively never. You cannot buy that reliability in one perfect drive at any price; you can have it for the cost of a second cheap one.
