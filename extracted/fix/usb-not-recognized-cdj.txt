CDJ NOT READING YOUR USB?
DO THIS, IN THIS ORDER
Short version: most "dead" USBs at the booth are a connection, slot, or format problem, not dead files.
The 60-second checks
 Reseating the drive and trying the other slot and the other player are
 assumed done. Listed for completeness, not as advice.
Wipe the connector, sweat and booth grime are real causes.
Give it 30+ seconds, big libraries mount slowly, especially on older CDJ-2000s.
If nobody's playing on it: power-cycle the player and try again.
Still nothing? The real causes
Wrong format: NTFS never works; exFAT only on newer firmware; GPT partition maps confuse most players. The universal answer is FAT32 + MBR.
Corrupt database: player sees the drive but shows an error or empty library → see E-8302.
Dying hardware: the drive doesn't mount on a computer either. Stop plugging it in, you're making recovery harder.
Power-hungry drives: some SSDs and hub-style drives draw more power than a CDJ port gives. Plain quality flash drives are safer.
If you play soon
With a laptop at the venue, you can usually rescue the night: copy your essential tracks to the computer, reformat the drive FAT32/MBR, copy the tracks back, and play them from the folder view. You lose cues and sync, you keep the gig. The emergency flow below walks you through it step by step, including the no-laptop route.
