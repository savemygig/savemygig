WAVEFORMS NOT LOADING?
Read this first
Short version: blank waveforms almost never stop a set. The audio plays fine, you are missing convenience, not music. Fix order below.
The three causes
Tracks were never analyzed. Files dragged manually (or imported and exported too fast) carry no waveform data. The player shows a slowly-building wave or nothing.
The drive is slow or dying. Waveform data loads from the USB; a struggling drive shows tracks instantly but waveforms crawl. Treat this as an early warning.
Database mismatch. Old player firmware + new Rekordbox versions occasionally skip detailed waveform data. Audio still plays.
At the venue
Play on. The wave draws in real time as the player reads the track, beatmatch by ear like the veterans did.
If waveforms crawl AND browsing lags: the drive is struggling. Don't trust it for the next gig - this is how those drives end.
At home
In Rekordbox: select your library → right-click → Analyze. Let it finish completely.
Re-export tonight's playlists in EXPORT mode; the analysis travels with the export.
Update player firmware and Rekordbox to the versions your gear supports.
Full-format (not quick) the suspect drive; if it crawls, retire it. Drives that don't do this →
