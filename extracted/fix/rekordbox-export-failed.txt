REKORDBOX EXPORT FAILED?
HERE'S THE RECOVERY
Short version: a failed or corrupt export is a database problem, not a music problem. Your tracks are almost always fine, the index the CDJ reads is broken. A clean re-export fixes most cases.
The two flavors
Export won't complete: hangs, errors mid-sync, or Rekordbox stops seeing the device. Usually a failing drive, a full drive, or a locked file. If the drive never appears in Rekordbox at all, that is a different problem, see Rekordbox not detecting the USB.
Export "worked" but the player shows nothing / errors: the database landed corrupt, classic after an unsafe eject. Often shows as E-8302 at the booth.
The recovery, in order
Close Rekordbox, unplug and replug the USB, reopen Rekordbox in EXPORT mode.
Check free space, an export onto a nearly-full drive corrupts silently.
If the device shows in Rekordbox: delete tonight's playlists from the device, re-sync them fresh, and eject with the eject button.
If the device errors or won't sync: copy any music you need off the drive, format it FAT32 + MBR, and export again to the clean drive.
Export small. Tonight's playlists only. A 2,000-track full-library export is where corruption lives.
Verify: eject, replug, reopen, the device should load with playlists intact before you leave the house.
Make exports bulletproof
Always eject from Rekordbox, then wait for the OS to release the drive.
Keep the library healthy: File → Library → Backup Library, monthly.
Back up the Rekordbox library to another drive or cloud.
Two identical USBs, exported together. The second one is why this article never matters again.
