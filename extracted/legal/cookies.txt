Cookie Policy
Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Part of the legal shelf
 Short version: this site sets no cookies until you accept analytics on
 the banner or sign in to an account. Signing in sets one strictly
 necessary cookie that keeps you signed in, nothing else. Analytics is
 optional and off by default. Decline and the site works exactly the same.
The one category of cookies we use
Analytics (optional, off by default). If you accept on
 the banner, we load Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC), which sets cookies
 beginning with _ga. We use it to see which
 pages actually help DJs and where people get stuck, nothing more. If you
 decline, Google Analytics is never loaded and these cookies are never
 set. Google describes its own processing in its privacy policy at
 policies.google.com/privacy.
 We set no advertising cookies and do
 no cross-site tracking, with or without consent.
The sign-in cookie (strictly necessary)
 If you register or sign in, we set one cookie named
 smg_s. It is what keeps this device signed
 in. It holds a signed session token, not a tracking ID, lasts 90 days,
 and is never used for analytics or advertising. Signing out or deleting
 your account removes it. Never sign in and this cookie is never set.
What lives in your browser instead
 The site stores some data in your browser's local storage. Local storage
 is not a cookie and never travels with your requests on its own. It
 stays on your device unless you ask us to move it: the Email list
 button sends your list to your inbox through Brevo, and account sync
 stores a copy on our servers so your other devices can load it. Both
 are described in the Privacy Policy. It
 holds your cookie choice itself (so the banner does not ask on every
 visit), your checklist (items, edits, ticks, notes), your registration
 on this device, including the email address and name you registered
 with, and a few small preferences such as dismissed notices.
 The site can also install a small offline cache (a service worker) so
 the rescue pages keep working in a basement booth with no signal. That
 cache contains our pages, nothing about you.
Changing your mind
 Clear this site's cookies in your browser and the banner asks again on
 your next visit. Careful with the nuclear option: clearing all site
 data also deletes your checklist from this device, so download or email
 your list first. You can also block cookies for this site entirely in
 your browser settings; the rescue flow does not need them.
