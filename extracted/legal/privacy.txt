LEGAL
PRIVACY, PLAINLY
Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Part of the legal shelf
 Short version: the rescue flow needs nothing from you. No account, no login, no files uploaded.
 Some free features (Advanced and Custom checklists, the printable list, emailing yourself a copy)
 ask you to register with an email address and a name, plus an optional Instagram handle you
 can leave empty. That is almost all the personal data we ever hold; an optional account adds
 one thing, a synced copy of your checklists, and its delete button removes everything.
Cookies and analytics
 We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages actually help and where people get stuck.
 It sets cookies, so we only load it after you accept on the banner.
 Decline and no analytics cookies are set, and the site works exactly the same.
 The full detail lives in the Cookie Policy.
 To change your mind later, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will ask again.
If you register
 Registration collects your email address, an artist name or first
 name so the site knows what to call you, and, only if you choose to share it, an
 artist Instagram handle. The email is used to unlock the registered
 features, to send you what you asked for (your checklist, the Emergency Card), and for the
 occasional note that might save a gig or help your career. We use double opt-in for the
 mailing list: you get a confirmation email first, and nothing is added to the list until
 you click it. When you submit the form we do store your details with Brevo straight away,
 marked as unconfirmed and outside every mailing list, so no campaign can ever reach them.
 Never click the link and you are never on the list; to have an unconfirmed record deleted,
 write to info@savemygig.com.
 If you share your Instagram handle, we use it for exactly what the form says: to follow
 your work from the Save My Gig account. We also receive an internal notification email for
 each registration so we can do that. The Instagram field is always optional, and leaving
 it empty changes nothing.
 This data is processed by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), acting as our
 processor. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone else. Every email has a one-click
 unsubscribe, and unsubscribing removes you. Your checklist itself (items, edits, ticks)
 is stored in your own browser on your own device, not on our servers, unless you create
 an account (next section).
If you create an account
 An account is optional and adds one thing: your checklists follow you across devices.
 Sign-in is your email address, proven either by Google sign-in or by a
 link we email you. There are no passwords, so there is no password of
 yours for anyone to steal. If you use Google, we receive your email address from Google
 and nothing else; we never see your Google password or your Google data.
 With an account, a copy of your checklists (the items, your edits, your ticks, your
 notes) is stored on our servers, hosted by Cloudflare, so your other
 devices can load it. The list on each device remains stored on that device either way.
Deleting your account is one button on the checklist page. It removes
 your account, every synced checklist, and your email address from our records,
 immediately, including the mailing-list contact. What stays is the copy of your list in
 your own browser, because that one belongs to your device, not to us.
If you send feedback
 The feedback form sends us what you write, plus your name, email and Instagram if you
 choose to leave them. It arrives as an email in our inbox, delivered by Brevo, and we use
 it to read it, reply if you asked for a reply, and follow you back if you left your
 handle. Want a message of yours deleted? Write to
 info@savemygig.com and it is gone.
Your rights
 If you are in the EU or UK, you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to
 correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to
 info@savemygig.com and we will action it.
 Our legal basis for the email list and the optional fields is your consent, which you can
 withdraw at any time; deleting your data removes your address, artist name and Instagram
 handle from our records. This applies worldwide, including under Brazil's LGPD: same
 address, same result.
What we never do
 No selling of data. No tracking beyond the analytics
 described above. We never see your music, your files, or your drives.
