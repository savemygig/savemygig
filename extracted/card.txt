THE EMERGENCY CARD
📱 Screenshot this page now, it works with no signal, in any booth, at 3am.
⚡ USB DEAD AT THE BOOTH
 Assumes you have already reseated it and tried the other deck. These are
 the four a good DJ still forgets under pressure.
FOLDERThe database is gone. The music is not.
LINKPlay off the other player’s drive.
RESTARTPower-cycle the dead player.
BORROWTake a drive off the DJ either side of you.
🔧 THE SACRED SETTINGS
Format: FAT32 · Scheme: MBR · Size: 32-64 GB
Mac: Disk Utility → Show All Devices → select the DRIVE → MS-DOS (FAT) + Master Boot Record
Music onto gig drives ONLY via Rekordbox EXPORT mode
Eject from Rekordbox AND the player (USB STOP). Every time.
🚫 NEVER (under pressure)
Never format a drive that still holds unrescued files.
Never say YES when a computer offers to format your gig drive.
Never keep re-plugging a drive that's failing, each mount can make recovery harder.
Never leave home with one drive. Two identical drives, two pockets.
