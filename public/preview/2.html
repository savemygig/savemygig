Most "dead" USBs at the booth are a connection or slot issue, not a dead drive. Fastest checks first:

  1. Pull the USB out and reseat it firmly. Wait 10 seconds before re-inserting.
  2. Try the other USB slot on the same player (if it has two).
  3. Try the other player. If it reads there, play from that deck and use Link.
  4. Check the USB tip for dirt or sweat, wipe it, try again.
