Most dead USBs at the booth are a connection or slot issue, not a dead drive. Fastest checks first.

01

Reseat the drive. Pull the USB out, wait ten seconds, push it back in firmly.

02

Try the other USB slot on the same player, if it has two.

03

Try the other player. If it reads there, play from that deck and use Link.

04

Wipe the USB tip. Dirt and sweat are real causes. Try again.
