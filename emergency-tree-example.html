One of the five doors, drawn at question level. Grey = question screen (one question, 2-4 pads). Amber = action screen (numbered steps, then one forward question). Green = outcome "you are playing". Red-edged = hand-off ladder (never a dead end). Reused existing screens are marked ↺.
